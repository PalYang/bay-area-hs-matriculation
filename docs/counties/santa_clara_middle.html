Santa Clara County Middle Schools — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Per-year T25 university matriculation for middle schools (and K-8 / 6-12 / K-12 middle programs) located in Santa Clara County. Each MS section derives a year-by-year T25% by taking the published feeder-HS mix and applying the corresponding HS T25% for that year (as estimated on the Santa Clara HS county page). For K-12 / 6-12 passthrough schools the HS's per-year T25 is used directly with a small "leakage" haircut.

Caveat: Middle schools almost never publish their own matriculation outcomes 4–6 years out. Per-year MS T25% here is an inference: feeder weights × the corresponding HS's per-year T25% (with COVID dip applied to 2021 ~85–90% of normal, and UCSD-expansion lift applied to 2024–2025 ~5–10% above normal where the feeder HSs themselves see those effects). See each MS's methodology block for the exact arithmetic.

Confidence legend: M measured (feeder weights from school-published "where they go" lists, AND feeder HSs publish per-year matriculation) · E estimated (one of {feeder weights, HS per-year T25} is solid, other inferred) · R rough estimate (feeder weights from district zoning maps or peer-school assumption) · N no surfaced feeder data; ballpark only.

Cross-cutting sources & methodology

Method: Per-year MS T25% = sum over feeder HSs of (feeder weight) × (HS per-year T25%), where:

  • Feeder weights come from middle_schools_ranked.html (which in turn references school-published "where they go" lists for the privates, CUSD/PAUSD/LASD attendance-area maps for the publics, and parent-forum / industry estimates for the chains and small independents).
  • HS per-year T25% comes from santa_clara.html for Santa Clara County feeder HSs (Lynbrook, Monta Vista, Cupertino, Homestead, Saratoga, Paly, Gunn, Castilleja, Harker, BASIS Indep SV, Pinewood, LAHS, MVHS, Mitty, Bellarmine, St Francis, Los Gatos, Leland-tier "other public"), and from san_mateo.html for the small Menlo/SHP/Crystal/Nueva private cross-feeds that show up in ISTP/Stratford/Helios/Stanford-OHS-adjacent K-8s.
  • COVID dip (2021): applied as 0.85–0.90 × steady-state where the feeder HS itself had a 2021 dip on its HS page. For schools whose HS feeders were flat through COVID (Bellarmine, Mitty, St Francis, Pinewood, LAHS, MVHS) the MS line is correspondingly flat.
  • UCSD-expansion lift (2024-2025): applied as +5-10% above the 2023 baseline where the feeder HS itself shows that lift on its HS page. Most pronounced for Cupertino-area MSs (Hyde, Joaquin Miller, Kennedy, Lawson, Cupertino MS) because Monta Vista/Cupertino/Homestead/Lynbrook all benefit from UCSD seat expansion.

Skipped schools: Stanford OHS MS — HQ in Redwood City (San Mateo County), covered on the san_mateo middle page. Crittenden / Graham (Mountain View Whisman SD), Sunnyvale SD K-8s, and Challenger School campuses were not present in middle_schools_ranked.html and so are not given inferred entries here (the source-data discipline rule: no fabrication).

Caveats: (1) MS→HS continuation rates are not surveyed annually for any of these schools; the feeder weights are stable over 2021–2025 within the noise of these estimates. (2) Per-year MS T25% inherits all of the underlying HS page's confidence band (typically ±2pp for M-confidence HSs, ±3-4pp for E-confidence, larger for R/N). (3) The "passthrough" K-12 schools (Harker MS, Castilleja MS, BASIS Indep SV MS, Pinewood MS) are dominated by their own US's per-year figure, so their per-year MS T25% is essentially the corresponding HS line on santa_clara.html with a small (≤2pp) leakage adjustment.
