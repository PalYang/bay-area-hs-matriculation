Contra Costa County — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Per-school annual T25 (US News national universities top-25) matriculation counts and rates for 29 Contra Costa County high schools (24 public, 5 private). Sorted by 5-year average T25% (highest first). T25 is taken as the union of US News rank 1–26 across the 2021–2026 ranking cycles to avoid arbitrary boundary effects (see methodology doc).

Read this first.
  • Per-year published data is rare in Contra Costa. Unlike Sequoia UHSD (Menlo-Atherton publishes per-college-per-year counts), Contra Costa public districts (Acalanes Union HSD, San Ramon Valley USD, Mt. Diablo USD, West Contra Costa USD, Liberty Union HSD) publish only multi-year combined matriculation lists or no lists at all. SRV's profile gives an alphabetical "2022-2023 College Placements" list with no counts.
  • Acalanes Union HSD (Lafayette/Orinda/Moraga/Walnut Creek) — the four-school district (Acalanes, Campolindo, Miramonte, Las Lomas) sits in some of the wealthiest Bay Area zip codes. After the 2026-04-26 UCSD-completeness revision, the top three (Campolindo, Miramonte ~16%; Acalanes ~15%) are comparable to some FUHSD or Sequoia UHSD schools and only modestly below Palo Alto USD; Las Lomas at ~10% sits in the next tier. UC-system (UCB+UCLA+UCSD) remains the dominant destination.
  • San Ramon Valley USD (Danville/San Ramon) — another high-SES district. After the 2026-04-26 UCSD-completeness revision: Dougherty Valley HS (~70% Asian, similar profile to Lynbrook) leads at ~14% T25; San Ramon Valley HS and Monte Vista at ~12%; California HS at ~9%. UC-system (UCB+UCLA+UCSD) is the dominant destination, with non-UC T25 share growing from Lamorinda toward East County.
  • Mt. Diablo USD comprehensives (Northgate, College Park, Concord, Mt. Diablo, Ygnacio Valley) have wide variation: Northgate ~11% T25 and College Park ~8% (both revised up 2026-04-26 to include UCSD as U.S. News T25), while Concord/Mt. Diablo/Ygnacio Valley remain near 1-2%.
  • West Contra Costa USD (Richmond/El Cerrito/Pinole/Hercules) — El Cerrito HS is the strongest (~3-4% T25); the rest are typically <1% T25.
  • East Contra Costa USD (Liberty Union HSD: Liberty/Heritage; Antioch USD: Deer Valley/Antioch; Pittsburg USD) — mostly <1% T25.
  • Catholic schools (De La Salle, Carondelet, Salesian) are systematically underweighted: many graduates choose Santa Clara, USF, USD, LMU, Pepperdine — none T25 — alongside Notre Dame.
  • Confidence tags: M measured (real per-year counts published), E estimated (scaled from multi-year list and class size), R rough (proxy from US News / Niche / GS only), N no usable data.

Estimation methodology

The 2021–2025 cells remain estimates derived from Athenian's 2024–25 multi-year college profile. The 2026 cell is measured from the complete class list in Athenian Magazine 2026, p. 29: 20 project-defined T25 matriculants among 87 graduates = 23.0%. The denominator includes three gap-year graduates because this metric is the share of the entire graduating class. The rolling 2022–2026 average is (19% + 19% + 20% + 20% + 23.0%) / 5 = 20.2%, displayed as ~20%. Confidence remains E for the five-year average because four years are estimated, while 2026 itself is M-quality.
