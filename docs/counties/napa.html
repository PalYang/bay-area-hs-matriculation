Napa County — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Napa County has only six high schools that send a meaningful number of grads to four-year colleges, and only one (Justin-Siena, the Catholic school) publishes any per-year matriculation. The Napa Valley USD comprehensives (Napa HS, Vintage HS, American Canyon HS) publish nothing useful. T25 volume is low countywide — even the strongest schools see at most a handful of T25 enrollments per year.

Methodology

St. Helena is a small (400-total enrollment) public IB school in the upper Napa Valley with an unusually affluent local catchment. Local archive (Napa Valley Register) has documented past UC Berkeley/UCLA grads from the IB cohort; on a class of ~100, even 1–2 T25 enrollments produces a relatively high share. Floor estimate of ~1.5% reflects this. No per-year published matriculation list, so all years are estimated.
