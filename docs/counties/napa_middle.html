Napa County Middle Schools — Year-by-year Inferred T25 %, 2021–2025

Per-school, per-year inferred T25 share for middle schools (and K-8s) in Napa County. The MS T25 % is derived from the high schools each MS feeds into, using the equation T25 %(MS, year) = Σ wi × T25 %(HSi, year). HS anchor values come from the Napa HS county page. See the middle-school methodology for the full inference equation and confidence-tier rules.

Caveat: Napa County is the smallest by HS count in the Bay Area: only six HSs that send a meaningful number of grads to four-year colleges and only one (Justin-Siena) publishes any per-year matriculation. Justin-Siena is 9-12 only (no MS), so the highest-yielding K-8 pipelines route through the Catholic K-8 cross-feeder to Justin-Siena. The Napa Valley USD comprehensives have ~1% T25 share, so even the dominant public MSs land near 1%. The COVID-class-of-2021 HS dip and the 2024-25 UCSD/UC-system admit-rate lift are inherited from the HS anchor values.

Confidence legend: M measured (rare for MS), E estimated (HS feeder is M-tier with one or two dominant feeders), R rough (multiple feeders, some E-tier HS anchors), N very rough (all R-tier feeders or unstable choice patterns). Schools sorted by 5-year-average inferred T25 share (descending).

Methodology & gaps

Catholic-K-8 to Catholic-HS pipeline; ~60% same-faith continuation is typical for parish K-8s. Justin-Siena is E-confidence on the HS side, so MS confidence is R. The big swing variable is the small handful of Apollinaris grads each year who attend Marin Catholic across the bridge (high-end Napa families occasionally send to Marin Catholic, ~14% T25 share); not modeled here because volume is low.
