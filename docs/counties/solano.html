Solano County — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Solano County is the lowest-T25-volume county in the immediate Bay Area. Its high schools are exclusively comprehensive publics serving Vallejo, Vacaville, Fairfield, and Benicia. There are no notable T25-feeder private schools in the county. None of the public high schools below publish per-college matriculation lists. Per-year T25 matriculation is in the 0–5 range for every school here, with the strongest signal coming from Vanden HS (Travis AFB-adjacent, military-officer-family demographic boost) and one-off Harvard/Berkeley individual stories from Vacaville HS in 2024.
