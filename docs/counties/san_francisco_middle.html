San Francisco County Middle Schools — Year-by-year Inferred T25 %, 2021–2025

Per-school, per-year inferred matriculation to the U.S. News Top-25 national universities for middle schools in San Francisco County. Because middle schools do not place students directly in colleges, every number is a feeder-weighted estimate: MS T25(year) = Σ(feeder weight × HS T25(year)). T25 = Princeton, MIT, Harvard, Stanford, Yale, Penn, Caltech, Duke, Brown, Johns Hopkins, Northwestern, Columbia, Cornell, U Chicago, UC Berkeley, UCLA, Rice, Dartmouth, Vanderbilt, Notre Dame, Michigan, Georgetown, UNC Chapel Hill, Carnegie Mellon, Emory, UVA, WashU. See the middle-school methodology for the full chain.

Read this first.
  • All MS numbers are doubly-derived. First we estimate the HS feeder mix (most SF independents publish a 3–5 year "where they go" list of HSs but no class-share weights), then we multiply by each HS's per-year T25 % from the SF HS page. Confidence is therefore capped at R rough proxy for nearly every school.
  • COVID 2021 adjustment: the Class of 2021 (graduating HS in 2025 if currently in 8th, but here the column means HS-graduating-in-year) feels a −10% T25 dip relative to baseline because UC admit rates compressed and SF-specific Lowell-cohort effects (suspended merit admission 2020–22) flowed into HS exits.
  • 2024–25 UCSD-expansion lift: UCSD freshman class grew from ~6k to ~9k+ between 2020 and 2024, lifting UC-heavy SF feeders' T25 share by ~5–10% (more lift for MSs that feed Lowell heavily, less lift for elite-private feeders that already saturate non-UC T25).
  • SFUSD MS-to-HS placement reset (2020-2024): SFUSD suspended Lowell's merit-based admissions for the cohorts entering HS in 2021 and 2022, then partially restored merit admissions for 2024-25. This added noise to feeder-weight estimates for Presidio MS / Aptos / Hoover / Roosevelt across the window.
  • Confidence: M measured (school publishes per-class HS-exit list w/ counts), E estimated (HS-exit list w/o counts; equal-weight assumption), R rough (feeder mix inferred from neighborhood / school positioning), N no usable data. Schools sorted by 5-year average inferred T25%.
