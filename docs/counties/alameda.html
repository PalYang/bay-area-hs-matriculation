Alameda County — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Detailed per-year T25 (U.S. News National Universities Top 25) matriculation data for high schools in Alameda County, California. Each section: year-by-year table where data exists, plus an estimation methodology block explaining sources, gaps, and how any estimate was derived. Sorted by 5-year average T25% (descending).

Read first. Per-year T25 matriculation data is rarely published in clean form. Most cells in the year tables below are marked "no per-year data" because the source schools publish only multi-year aggregates (4–5 yr lists), aggregate "where do our grads go" pages, or nothing at all. The 5-year average column is the most reliable figure, taken from the parent all-schools ranking; per-year breakouts are estimates anchored on UC source-school CSVs (UCB/UCLA admit and enrolled by feeder HS), CDE public profiles, school-paper reporting, and known multi-year aggregates. T25 reference list = U.S. News National Universities Top 25 ~2024 cycle (UCB and UCLA count; LACs do not). See per-school methodology blocks for the exact reasoning chain.
Selection bias dominates the top of the list. College Prep, Head-Royce, Mission San Jose, and Piedmont admit/enroll students who are already on a strong trajectory. Their T25 % reflects who they enroll as much as what they teach. The bottom of the list (McClymonds, Castlemont, Tennyson, etc.) reflects deeply under-resourced communities — T25 placement is not a meaningful signal of school quality at those schools and we include them only for completeness.

1. College Preparatory School (CPS) — Oakland Private indep / coed / ~80 grads · 5-yr avg 47.8% M

The single highest-T25 anchor school in the East Bay and one of the deepest data sets in the entire Bay Area survey. The school publishes a five-year cumulative matriculation list with per-college counts, which is what makes the M-tag possible. Five-year cumulative T25 (over ~400 grads): UChicago 24, UCB 16, Georgetown 15, WashU 14, Harvard 13, Cornell 12, Brown 10, Stanford 10, Dartmouth 9, UCLA 9, Yale 8, Northwestern 8, Penn 8, CMU 8, Rice 7, Duke 6, JHU 6, Princeton 4, Michigan 4 = 191 / 400 = 47.8%. (No reported MIT, Caltech, Columbia, Notre Dame, Vanderbilt, Emory, UVA in the counted period.)

Methodology & sources

Source: college-prep.org list-detail (5-year cumulative matriculation list with per-college counts). The school does NOT publish a per-class breakout — they publish a single 5-year aggregate. Estimation: take the 5-year T25 sum (191) and divide by 5 classes (~80/yr) to get ~38 T25 matriculants per year, ~48%. Class sizes have been ~80 throughout 2021–25. T25 reference list excludes liberal arts colleges (Pomona, Williams, Swarthmore, Amherst, Harvey Mudd) — which would push CPS materially higher because they send substantial cohorts to top LACs each year.

4. The Quarry Lane School — Dublin Private indep STEM / ~78 grads · 5-yr avg ~17% R

Quarry Lane publishes 5-year ACCEPTANCE counts (UCB 61, UCLA 47, CMU 20, Michigan 19, Cornell 15, etc. = ~271 T25 acceptances over ~390 grads in 5 years), not matriculation counts. At a typical ~4× accept-to-matriculation ratio for selective schools, this implies ~68 matriculations / 390 grads ≈ 17%. The conversion ratio is the largest source of uncertainty — actual matriculations could be 50–85 / 390 = 13–22%.

Methodology overview (applies to all sections above):
  • T25 reference list: U.S. News National Universities Top 25 ~2024 cycle. UCB and UCLA count as T25; LACs (Pomona, Williams, Swarthmore, Amherst, Harvey Mudd) do NOT, which materially understates the elite-college rate at College Prep, Head-Royce, and Piedmont in particular.
  • Anchor sources (in order of weight): (1) school-published per-year matriculation lists with per-college counts (only CPS hits this in Alameda); (2) school-published multi-year aggregates with counts; (3) UC source-school CSV (UCB and UCLA enrolled by feeder HS — published yearly by the UC Office of the President); (4) school profile PDFs with destination lists (no counts); (5) student newspaper coverage; (6) Niche / US News aggregate proxies; (7) peer-school analogy.
  • Confidence tags: M measured (±2pp), E estimated (±5pp), R rough (±10pp), N no usable data.
  • Per-year breakouts where no per-year data exists: we apportion the multi-year aggregate evenly across years and flag the row with no per-year data. We do not invent year-over-year movement that the data doesn't show.
  • Catholic schools (Bishop O'Dowd, Saint Mary's College HS): grads systematically prefer Notre Dame, Santa Clara, USF, USD (only Notre Dame is in T25), so absolute T25 share looks low even at strong, large Catholic schools. Read those rows in the context of the broader selective-college pipeline, not just T25.
  • Selection bias: the top of this list (CPS, Head-Royce, MSJ, Piedmont) admits/enrolls students already on a strong trajectory. The numbers reflect who enrolls as much as what's taught.
