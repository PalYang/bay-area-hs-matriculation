Sonoma County — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Sonoma County's high schools are overwhelmingly comprehensive publics serving Santa Rosa, Petaluma, and the wine-country towns. Only two schools in the county publish anything resembling a per-year matriculation list (Cardinal Newman annually; Sonoma Academy as a 5-year roll-up), and even those omit per-college counts in most years. For the great majority of schools below, no per-year, per-college matriculation is published. Where that is the case, a plate-tectonic-slow estimate is given from the school's CDE college-going rate, total UC admit/enroll volume from the UC source-school dashboard, and the typical conversion rate of "T25 admit" to "T25 enroll" in this region (most cross-admits stay in-state at UCs). Floor estimates of 0–3 T25 matriculants per year per school are the norm here; that is not a research failure, it is the actual signal.

Methodology

Sonoma Academy publishes a single 2021–2025 cumulative roll-up at sonomaacademy.org/college-matriculations, not per-year data. T25 universities appearing on the matriculation list across the 5-year window: Princeton, Harvard, Yale, Stanford, Columbia, Penn, Northwestern, JHU, Cornell, Dartmouth, UC Berkeley, UCLA, Rice, Carnegie Mellon, Notre Dame. With ~350 grads over 5 years and the school's own published claim of ~16.88% to T50 / ~4.71% to T25 (per FindingSchool aggregation), the total T25 count is ~50 over five years, ~10/year. The 5-year share lands near 14–16% if you count UC Berkeley + UCLA generously; lower (~5%) if you exclude UC. Tabling the higher reading because Sonoma Academy is a heavy UC sender and most cross-admits do enroll. Distribution across years is assumed flat — small-N volatility means any year can swing ±3 students.

Methodology

2025-26 College Admissions Profile (downloaded from cardinalnewman.org via finalsite resources) lists matriculations with parenthetical counts. Class of 2025 had 108 seniors. Confirmed T25 enrollments with counts: Stanford (1), Cornell (1), Duke (1), UC Berkeley (1), UNC Chapel Hill (1). UCLA, Northwestern, Penn appear without counts in the historical lists. The class size dropped sharply from ~149 (typical) to 108 in 2025, consistent with the school recovering from 2017 Tubbs Fire enrollment dips and post-COVID class compression. Earlier years (2021–2024) extrapolate from the 2025 measured rate (4.6%) plus a small adjustment because Newman's earlier classes were larger; ~5 T25 enrollments per year is the modal best guess. Conversion confidence is moderate-low because the school's own profile only began listing parenthetical counts recently.

Methodology

2023-2024 School Profile PDF lists "College & University Attendance (last 2 years)" without counts. T25 universities appearing in the 2022–2024 list: Stanford, Cornell, Harvard, MIT, Penn, Brown, U Michigan, WashU, UC Berkeley, UCLA — that's 10 distinct T25 institutions for ~800 graduates over 2 years, suggesting roughly 12–15 total T25 matriculations across the two years (~6–7/year). National Merit results from the profile (1 winner in 2021, multiple semis/finals across years, only 2 semis in 2024) confirm a thin top-end. Carrillo is the strongest comprehensive public in Sonoma County and the only one with credible regular HYPSM matriculation, but the share is small relative to enrollment.
