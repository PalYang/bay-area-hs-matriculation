Sonoma County Middle Schools — Year-by-year Inferred T25 %, 2021–2025

Per-school, per-year inferred T25 share for middle schools (and K-8s) in Sonoma County. The MS T25 % is derived from the high schools each MS feeds into, using the equation T25 %(MS, year) = Σ wi × T25 %(HSi, year). HS anchor values come from the Sonoma HS county page. See the middle-school methodology for the full inference equation and confidence-tier rules.

Caveat: Sonoma County has very few private K-8s and most public middle schools feed into low-T25-share comprehensive HSs. Most rows below land in the 0.3–3% range. Schools with inferred T25 < 1% are still listed where the public-school role is structurally important; the lowest-yield rural K-8s are folded into a residual note. The COVID-class-of-2021 HS dip and the 2024–25 UCSD/UC-system admit-rate lift are inherited from the HS anchor values.

Confidence legend: M measured (rare for MS — would require a published per-college MS-grad tracking list), E estimated (HS feeder is M-tier with one or two dominant feeders), R rough (multiple feeders, some E-tier HS anchors), N very rough (all R-tier feeders or unstable choice patterns). Per the methodology, MS confidence is one tier worse than the dominant HS feeder. Schools sorted by 5-year-average inferred T25 share (descending).

Methodology & gaps

The threshold for inclusion in this page is inferred T25 ≥ 1% OR structural importance (largest public MS in a district). Schools below threshold are noted here for completeness; their inferred T25 % can be reconstructed by applying the feeder equation against the published HS T25 % values on the Sonoma HS county page.
