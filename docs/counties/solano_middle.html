Solano County Middle Schools — Year-by-year Inferred T25 %, 2021–2025

Per-school, per-year inferred T25 share for middle schools (and K-8s) in Solano County. The MS T25 % is derived from the high schools each MS feeds into, using the equation T25 %(MS, year) = Σ wi × T25 %(HSi, year). HS anchor values come from the Solano HS county page. See the middle-school methodology for the full inference equation and confidence-tier rules.

Caveat: Solano is the lowest-T25-volume Bay Area county. There are no notable T25-feeder private schools in the county. All HS T25 anchors are R-tier (no school-published per-college matriculation lists). Most rows below land in the 0.4–1% range. Schools below ~1% are still included where they are the largest in their district. The COVID-class-of-2021 HS dip and the 2024-25 UCSD/UC-system admit-rate lift are inherited from the HS anchor values.

Confidence legend: M measured (rare for MS), E estimated (HS feeder is M-tier with one or two dominant feeders), R rough (multiple feeders, some E-tier HS anchors), N very rough (all R-tier feeders or unstable choice patterns). Schools sorted by 5-year-average inferred T25 share (descending).
