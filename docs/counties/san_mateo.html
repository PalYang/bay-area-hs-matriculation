San Mateo County — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Per-school annual T25 (US News national universities top-25) matriculation counts and rates for 24 San Mateo County high schools (16 public, 8 private). Sorted by 5-year average T25% (highest first). T25 is taken as the union of US News rank 1–26 across the 2021–2026 ranking cycles to avoid arbitrary boundary effects (see methodology doc).

Read this first.
  • Most public comprehensives don't publish per-year T25 counts. San Mateo Union HSD school profiles publish single-year matriculation lists (alphabetical, no counts) for the most recent class only. Sequoia UHSD (Carlmont, M-A, Sequoia, Woodside) is the strongest exception: Menlo-Atherton's "Graduate Destinations 22-25" PDF gives per-year counts for 2022–2025.
  • Private school profiles publish multi-year combined lists, not annual counts. Sacred Heart Prep, Menlo School, Crystal Springs, Nueva, Notre Dame Belmont, and Woodside Priory all release rolling 3–5 year matriculation lists. Per-year breakdowns are interpolated from class-size, profile language ("typically 5–10% to T25"), and matriculation maps.
  • UC source-school CSV is the per-year backstop. For schools without published matriculation lists, UC enrollee counts at UCB+UCLA can be scaled to T25 using the typical Bay Area ratio (UCB+UCLA captures ~50–70% of T25 matriculations for most public comprehensives, since most other T25 schools are private and out-of-state). UC CSV access was rate-limited during this build — cited where used.
  • Catholic schools are systematically underweighted. Mercy Burlingame and Notre Dame Belmont graduates often choose Santa Clara, USF, Loyola Marymount, USD — none of which are T25. Their absolute T25% looks low even when academic outcomes are strong.
  • Confidence tags: M measured (real per-year counts published), E estimated (scaled from multi-year list and class size), R rough (proxy from US News / Niche / GS only), N no usable data.
