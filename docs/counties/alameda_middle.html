Alameda County Middle Schools — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Detailed per-year T25 (U.S. News National Universities Top 25) matriculation data for middle schools, K-8s, and 6-12 / K-12 middle programs in Alameda County, California. Each section: year-by-year table where data exists, plus methodology block explaining feeder weighting, sources, and how any estimate was derived. Sorted by 5-year average T25% (descending).

Read first. Middle schools rarely publish college-matriculation data because their graduates haven't gone to college yet — by definition, MS T25 % is a downstream proxy derived from where their grads go for high school, then where those high schools place. Computation is: MS T25(year) = Σ (feeder weight × HS T25(year)). Per-year HS T25 numbers come from the Alameda HS county page. Feeder weights come from school district enrollment patterns and (where available) school-published "where our 8th graders go" data. K-12 / 6-12 passthrough schools (BASIS Independent Fremont, The Quarry Lane) inherit their HS's per-year data directly with a small attrition haircut. COVID adjustment and UCSD-completeness adjustment are applied via the underlying HS per-year curves, so 2021 numbers are typically slightly depressed and 2024-25 slightly elevated relative to the 5-yr midpoint.
Confidence is universally low. Almost no Alameda County middle school publishes a "where do our 8th graders go" breakdown that names HS destinations with counts. Feeder weights below are estimates anchored on (a) district enrollment-area mappings (e.g., Hopkins JH → Mission San Jose HS is the published district feeder pattern), (b) school-published "where they go" lists when available (Black Pine Circle, Berkwood Hedge), and (c) reasonable defaults (Catholic K-8 → same-network HS at ~50% rate). Confidence is R (rough estimate) or N (no data) for nearly every school. The 5-yr average column is the most reliable per-row figure — single-year breakouts are largely interpolation from the underlying HS per-year curve.

1. BASIS Independent Fremont MS Private indep K-12 / STEM-focused / 5-12 MS-US continuum / Fremont · 5-yr avg ~48% E

Direct passthrough K-12: BASIS Independent Fremont's middle school (grades 5-8) feeds straight into the same school's upper school (grades 9-12). Attrition between 8th and 9th grade exists but is small — most BASIS MS families are in the program because of the BASIS US trajectory. T25 % therefore inherits the upper school's per-year T25 % almost in full. See the BASIS Independent Fremont HS section on the Alameda HS page for the underlying HS per-year computation (Class of 2025 acceptances blog × 90% STEM-affluent-secular yield, ~48% midpoint).

Methodology & sources

Computation: T25(year=Y) = HS T25(year=Y+4) since the 8th-grade exit cohort graduates HS four years later. We assume ~95% MS-to-US continuation (per BASIS-brand norm; the few who exit go to FUHSD publics or other privates and would land in the ~10-25% T25 zone). The remaining 5% adds little to the T25 share, so the practical computation is "use HS T25 directly." Per-year HS T25 values come from alameda.html#basisfremont, which itself is anchored on the Class of 2025 acceptances blog (29% T15-accepted, ~50-55% T25-accepted, 92% T50) converted with a 90% yield for STEM-focused affluent secular schools. Class size ~50-60 per BASIS-brand growth. Confidence E because the upstream HS data is E and the passthrough assumption is solid for K-12 BASIS schools.

Methodology & sources

Sources: sjndhs.org; Bishop O'Dowd, alameda.html; Alameda HS, alameda.html. Standard Catholic K-8 same-campus-pull pattern: ~50% retention at affiliated HS plus a meaningful Catholic-other slice (O'Dowd, the much larger East Bay Catholic) plus public exit. Catholic college-preference effect (kids preferring Notre Dame, Santa Clara, USF, USD over UC Berkeley) caps T25 % at modest levels even for the higher-tuition Catholic exits.

Methodology summary & cross-cutting caveats.
  • Per-year computation. MS T25(year=Y) = Σ (feeder weight × HS T25(year=Y+4)). The Y+4 offset is the standard 8th-grade-to-12th-grade lag.
  • HS per-year T25 source. All underlying HS T25 numbers come from alameda.html with COVID-2021 dip and UCSD-2024-expansion lift baked in.
  • Feeder weight source priority. (1) School-published "where they go" lists (Black Pine Circle); (2) district attendance-area maps (Hopkins, Walters, Horner, Albany MS, Piedmont MS, BUSD MSs); (3) peer-school assumptions (Berkwood Hedge, Beth Jacob, EBIA, Edna Brewer); (4) stated K-12 passthrough (BASIS Fremont, Quarry Lane).
  • Catholic K-8 default. ~50% same-network HS retention + ~25% other Catholic + ~25% public exit. SJND Elementary follows this template.
  • Confidence inflation hazard. Even when a feeder mapping is high-confidence (Hopkins → MSJ), the underlying HS T25 % itself has ±5pp variance because per-T25 itemized matriculation lists are essentially never published for Bay Area high schools. Therefore no MS row exceeds confidence E, and most are R or N.
  • Schools deliberately not given separate sections: Tehiyah Day School (El Cerrito, Contra Costa — covered there); Stratford Newark/Fremont and Challenger Newark/Fremont (rolled into the chain aggregates on the Santa Clara MS page); Mission San Jose Elementary (K-6, not a middle school); Centerville JH (folded into the Horner row as a near-duplicate FUSD American/Washington feeder).
