Per-school, per-year matriculation to the U.S. News Top-25 national universities for high schools in Marin County. T25 = Princeton, MIT, Harvard, Stanford, Yale, Penn, Caltech, Duke, Brown, Johns Hopkins, Northwestern, Columbia, Cornell, U Chicago, UC Berkeley, UCLA, Rice, Dartmouth, Vanderbilt, Notre Dame, Michigan, Georgetown, UNC Chapel Hill, Carnegie Mellon, Emory, UVA, WashU.

Caveat: Most Marin private schools publish multi-year aggregate matriculation lists with counts; public Tam Union HSD schools publish profiles with limited college breakdowns. Where a school does not break out per-year counts, we list "no data" and infer per-year averages from the aggregate. Confidence: M measured (PDF/profile w/ counts), E estimated (aggregate divided), R rough proxy, N no data. Schools sorted by 5-year average T25%.
