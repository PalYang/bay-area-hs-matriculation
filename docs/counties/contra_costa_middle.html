Contra Costa County — Middle Schools, Year-by-year Inferred T25, 2021–2025

Per-school annual inferred T25 (US News national universities top-25) matriculation rates for 19 Contra Costa County middle schools. Sorted by 5-year average inferred T25% (highest first). For K-12 / 6-12 schools we use direct passthrough from the school's own HS section (see Contra Costa HS year-by-year); for K-8 and standalone public MSs we compute MS T25(year) = Σ(feeder weight × HS T25(year)) per the middle-school methodology page.

Read this first.
  • Triple-estimation chain. MS feeder weight × HS T25 estimate × matriculation conversion. Confidence is one tier worse than the dominant HS feeder, except K-12 / 6-12 schools (M for direct passthrough).
  • Per-year MS rates track per-year HS rates. The 2024-2025 figures incorporate the UCSD-into-T25 lift that benefits the dominant UC-yielding feeders (Acalanes, Campolindo, Miramonte, Las Lomas, SRVHS, Monte Vista, Cal HS, Dougherty Valley, Northgate, College Park). Dougherty Valley benefits disproportionately because its highly-Asian demographic is a heavy UCSD admit pool.
  • AUHSD MSs feed 1-to-1 into AUHSD HSs. Each Lamorinda MS feeds essentially one HS: Stanley → Acalanes (Lafayette), Joaquin Moraga → Campolindo (Moraga), OIS → Miramonte (Orinda), Foothill → Las Lomas/Acalanes (Walnut Creek). This makes per-year MS T25 a near-clone of the HS per-year T25.
  • SRVUSD MSs have more spread. Charlotte Wood, Diablo Vista, Stone Valley feed SRV/Monte Vista; Gale Ranch, Iron Horse, Pine Valley, Windemere Ranch feed Cal HS and Dougherty Valley with varying splits.
  • Tehiyah Day School closed in 2024 — historical estimates preserved here for context but the school no longer enrolls.
  • Confidence tags: M measured (passthrough or per-year published), E estimated (±6pp), R rough (±10pp), N very rough (±15pp).
