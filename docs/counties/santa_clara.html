Santa Clara County — Year-by-year T25 Matriculation, 2021–2025

Detailed per-year T25 university matriculation data for high schools in Santa Clara County (Bay Area, California). Each school's section includes a year-by-year table (where data exists) and an estimation methodology block explaining sources and gaps. Schools are sorted by 5-year average T25% descending.

Caveat: Per-year T25 matriculation data is not consistently published. Most rows will be "no per-year data" with the estimate derived from multi-year aggregates or proxies (UC source-school CSV, school profile PDFs). See each school's methodology block for specifics. T25 is defined as US News Top 25 national universities + the most-cited LACs (typically MIT, Stanford, Ivies, UChicago, Duke, Northwestern, Johns Hopkins, Caltech, UC Berkeley, UCLA, Vanderbilt, Rice, Notre Dame, WashU, Emory, Georgetown, Carnegie Mellon, USC, Michigan, UVA, NYU, etc.).

Confidence legend: M measured (school publishes verbatim per-year matriculation lists for most years) · E estimated from one or two solid data points or multi-year aggregates · R rough estimate from proxies (UC CSV, US News, news clippings) · N no usable data; ballpark only.

Estimation methodology

Castilleja publishes a rolling multi-year matriculation list on its school profile PDF (no per-year tabular breakdown). No journalistic per-year coverage was found for 2021–2024.

Cross-cutting sources & methodology

Primary data sources used across schools (in order of priority):

  • School profile PDFs / counseling pages — the gold standard for per-year matriculation data. Available with per-year detail for: Harker, Paly, Gunn (CCC pages with year columns), Lynbrook (FUHSD profile), MVHS (FUHSD profile), CHS (FUHSD profile), HHS (FUHSD profile), Saratoga (LGSUHSD profile), Castilleja (multi-year aggregate), BASIS Independent SV (recent profile), Pinewood (multi-year aggregate), Presentation (recent profile).
  • UC source-school CSV — universityofcalifornia.edu publishes per-school freshman applicant/admit/enrollee data for every UC campus by year. This is the cleanest ground-truth proxy for the UCB+UCLA components of T25 across all California public schools and is used as the primary cross-check throughout. Berkeley and UCLA are the only T25 institutions in this dataset; combined, they typically account for 50–75% of a Bay Area school's T25 matriculants, so this proxy is highly informative.
  • Local press — Mercury News (San Jose), Palo Alto Online, Almanac (Menlo Park / Atherton). Useful for occasional senior-signing-day stories and Stanford/Ivy headcounts. Most coverage is incidental rather than systematic.
  • Reddit / r/ApplyingToCollege / r/Cupertino — useful for current-year color (informal student polls in senior threads), but not citable as a primary data source. Used here only to sanity-check direction-of-change in T25 share year-over-year.
  • Niche / US News — multi-year aggregates, not per-year. Used as floor checks only.

T25 boundary calls: The T25 set is the US News Top 25 national universities for the relevant year, with minor adjustments for cross-year volatility (UMich, UVA, UCSD, NYU cycle in/out). For LACs, only Williams/Amherst/Swarthmore/Pomona/Wellesley/Bowdoin/Carleton-tier are counted when a school's matriculation list separates them from broader LAC counts. Boundary uncertainty is typically ~±1–2 percentage points.

What's NOT here: Charter schools serving Santa Clara County that are part of larger networks (KIPP San Jose, Summit Public Schools campuses) are omitted because their T25 pipelines are negligible (<1%). Continuation schools and alternative high schools are also omitted.
