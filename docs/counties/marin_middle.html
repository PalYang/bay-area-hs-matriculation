Marin County Middle Schools — Year-by-year Inferred T25 %, 2021–2025

Per-school, per-year inferred matriculation to the U.S. News Top-25 national universities for middle schools in Marin County. Because middle schools do not place students directly in colleges, every number is a feeder-weighted estimate: MS T25(year) = Σ(feeder weight × HS T25(year)). T25 = Princeton, MIT, Harvard, Stanford, Yale, Penn, Caltech, Duke, Brown, Johns Hopkins, Northwestern, Columbia, Cornell, U Chicago, UC Berkeley, UCLA, Rice, Dartmouth, Vanderbilt, Notre Dame, Michigan, Georgetown, UNC Chapel Hill, Carnegie Mellon, Emory, UVA, WashU. See the middle-school methodology for the full chain.

Read this first.
  • All MS numbers are doubly-derived. First we estimate the HS feeder mix (Marin independents publish 3–5 year "where they go" lists of HSs but no class-share weights), then we multiply by each HS's per-year T25 % from the Marin HS page. Confidence is therefore capped at R rough proxy for nearly every school.
  • Marin's HS exit pattern is bimodal. Independent K-8s (MCDS, Mt. Tam, Marin Horizon, St. Mark's) feed Branson + Marin Academy heavily (~40-60% combined to private). Public-track MSs (Mill Valley, Del Mar, Hall, Davidson, San Rafael) feed Tam UHSD (Tamalpais HS, Redwood HS, Archie Williams) with virtually no private exit. Brandeis Marin sits in between.
  • COVID 2021 adjustment: −10% for HS classes graduating in 2021 (UC admit rate compression hit Tam UHSD's Cal Poly SLO / UC pipeline).
  • 2024–25 UCSD-expansion lift: +5–10% on Tam UHSD-feeder and Marin Catholic-feeder weights. Independent-school feeders (Branson 51%, MA 24%) are already saturated on non-UC T25 so see less lift.
  • No K-12 passthrough schools in Marin. All Marin K-8 privates exit to a separate HS at age 13–14; San Domenico is the only K-12 (and is excluded here because it has its own MS-component already on the HS page).
  • Confidence: M measured (school publishes per-class HS-exit list w/ counts), E estimated (HS-exit list w/o counts), R rough (feeder mix inferred from neighborhood / school positioning), N no usable data. Schools sorted by 5-year average inferred T25%.
