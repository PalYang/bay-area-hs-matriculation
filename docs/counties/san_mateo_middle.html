San Mateo County — Middle Schools, Year-by-year Inferred T25, 2021–2025

Per-school annual inferred T25 (US News national universities top-25) matriculation rates for 21 San Mateo County middle schools. Sorted by 5-year average inferred T25% (highest first). For K-12 / 6-12 schools we use direct passthrough from the school's own HS section (see San Mateo HS year-by-year); for K-8 and standalone public MSs we compute MS T25(year) = Σ(feeder weight × HS T25(year)) per the middle-school methodology page.

Read this first.
  • Triple-estimation chain. MS feeder weight × HS T25 estimate × matriculation conversion. Confidence is one tier worse than the dominant HS feeder, except K-12 / 6-12 schools (M for direct passthrough).
  • Per-year MS rates track per-year HS rates. The 2021 figures reflect a COVID-era class-of-25-acceptances dip; 2024-2025 figures incorporate the UCSD-into-T25 lift that benefits the dominant UC-yielding feeders (M-A, Aragon, Carlmont, Burlingame).
  • Catholic K-8s are systematically underweighted. Most St. Charles / St. Catherine / St. Robert's / All Souls / St. Veronica grads continue to NDB, Mercy Burlingame, or Junipero Serra — all of which are themselves Catholic-underweighted. The arithmetic compounds.
  • Feeder-weight drift. The Sequoia UHSD school-of-residence assignment between Carlmont/M-A/Sequoia/Woodside has shifted post-2020 with the addition of TIDE Academy and choice patterns; weights below are the dominant-feeder approximation, not a 5-year average.
  • Confidence tags: M measured (passthrough or per-year published), E estimated (±6pp), R rough (±10pp), N very rough (±15pp).
