# Graduate Profile Audit — Top 10 Bay Area High Schools

Goal: sanity-check current T25% estimates against publicly searchable graduate data (school matriculation lists, news features, alumni pages). This audit deliberately uses individual graduate-to-college matches where available; where only aggregate school-published or aggregator data exists, that is noted.

Time-boxed audit (~60 min). Web sources cited per school. LinkedIn snippet evidence flagged as low-confidence. No graduates fabricated — schools with zero individual hits are flagged.

Note on T25 definition: aggregator stats from findingschool.com use "top 25 US universities and colleges" (combining T25 national universities + ~T25 LACs / similar), broadly consistent with the project's T25 definition. Tier shapes are useful as a directional cross-check even if not identical.

### Suggested revision
- No strong revision warranted on this evidence; possibly slight downward to ~40-45% if treating BASIS-wide T25 acceptance as the ceiling. Recommend leaving at ~45% unless other evidence available.

## Top 3 most consequential potential revisions

1. **Branson School: 50% → ~35%** (high consequence; #1 ranked school). School's own 4-year matriculation list and aggregator both indicate ~33%, not 50%. The "Ivy schools = 1 grad each over 4 years" detail (Harvard 1, Princeton 1) is a strong signal that current estimate is significantly overstated. **HIGH CONFIDENCE.**

2. **Castilleja School: 42% → ~30%** (medium-high consequence; #4 ranked). Aggregator says 22.95% T25; even after adjusting for likely under-counting of women's LACs (Smith/Wellesley/Barnard), realistic is probably 28-32%. **MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.**

3. **Lick-Wilmerding HS: 33% → ~25-28%** (medium consequence; #8 ranked). Aggregator (20.9%) is meaningfully below project (33%); deserves deeper look. **LOW-MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.**

## Caveats and methodology notes
- Findingschool.com's "top 25" aggregator does not perfectly match the project's T25 definition; it tends to undercount LACs. Adjustments above attempt to compensate.
- Individual graduate-to-college matches are sparse in public sources for almost all schools (most do not publish them; Palo Alto Online uniquely publishes them for Bay Area private schools, but rate-limiting prevented full extraction during this audit).
- BASIS publishes acceptance %, not matriculation %; these are not directly comparable to project T25 matriculation estimates.
- Nueva's most recent PDF (23-24 College Profile) is binary-only via WebFetch; sister agent should retry with a working PDF parser.
- The Castilleja Class of 2023 Palo Alto Online article (https://www.paloaltoonline.com/news/2023/06/02/castilleja-school-9/) is known to contain a full graduate-college list and is a high-value target for a follow-up audit when rate limiting clears.
