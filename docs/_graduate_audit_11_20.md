# Graduate Audit — Bay Area HS Ranks 11–20

**Audited:** 2026-04-26 (60-min time-box)
**Methodology:** Cross-checked online sources (school official matriculation pages/PDFs, Niche, Findingschool, kron4 Ivy report, Cardinal Education, Stanford Daily, Wikipedia, school blogs) against current T25 estimates in `docs/all_schools_ranked.html`.

**Note on rank order:** User-supplied list was off-by-one vs `all_schools_ranked.html`. Actual rank 11–20 per the file is: Head-Royce (11), Crystal Springs Uplands (12), UHS/SFUHS (13), Sacred Heart Prep (14), Marin Academy (15), Mission San Jose (16), Lynbrook (17), Monta Vista (18), Pinewood (19), Paly (20). User also asked me to audit BASIS SV (actual rank 10) and to verify Sequoia / Menlo-Atherton placement. Both verified: neither is in the 11–20 window (Sequoia rank ≈ low-50s; Menlo-Atherton rank ≈ low-30s based on table grep). Sister agent at 1-10 likely already covered BASIS SV; I include it here as the user asked.

**Niche caveat:** Niche's "% to T25" appears to be self-reported alumni-survey-based and is wildly under-stated for most private schools (e.g., Crystal Springs shows 6.58% T25 on Niche vs. school's own data showing ~25 of 26 T25 schools with matriculations). Niche numbers are **not used as primary evidence** below — only as a sanity bound or where it confirms other sources.

## 10. BASIS Independent Silicon Valley — current ~30%

**Data points:**
- BASIS SV Class of 2025: **54% T25 acceptance, 91% T50 acceptance** (school blog / press release). Class size ~90.
- Class of 2025 admits: **5 Stanford, 2 MIT, 1 Princeton, 1 Harvard, 1 UChicago, 13 total Ivy admits** (school blog).
- Class of 2024 acceptance list includes Princeton, Columbia, Harvard, MIT, RISD.
- Sister school BASIS Fremont was independently revised to ~48% T25 matriculation using same yield logic (90%) in `all_schools_ranked.html`; BASIS SV profile is nearly identical (STEM-focused, K-12 affluent secular).

**Aggregation:** 54% T25 acceptance × 90% yield = **~49% T25 matriculation** — almost exactly the BASIS Fremont number, much higher than current 30%.

**Verdict:** **STRONG REVISION SUGGESTED** — current 30% is roughly half the implied figure. Estimate has not been corrected to match sister school's revision (lessons doc flagged this; correction not yet propagated).

**Revision suggestion:** ~30% → **~45-48%** T25, putting BASIS SV at peer level with BASIS Fremont (rank would jump well up into top ~3-5).

## 12. Crystal Springs Uplands — current ~28%

**Data points:**
- **Stanford Daily (2022):** "Crystal Springs Uplands sent **7 of ~90 seniors** to Stanford in the 2020-21 cycle" = **~7.8% to Stanford alone in one year.**
- School lists matriculations at 25 of 26 T25 schools (per current `all_schools_ranked.html` tooltip).
- School's own page confirms bolded matriculations across Harvard, Yale, Princeton, Columbia, Cornell, Dartmouth, Brown, Penn, Stanford, MIT, Duke, Northwestern, JHU, Vanderbilt, Rice, WashU, Emory, UC Berkeley, UCLA.
- Niche says 6.58% T25 (clearly wrong — contradicted by Stanford Daily's 7.8% to Stanford alone).
- Class of 2011 matriculation page also showed Stanford + Princeton matriculations historically.

**Aggregation:** If 7.8% goes to Stanford in a strong year and 25 of 26 T25 institutions receive matriculations, total T25 likely 28-35%. Current 28% is at the low end of plausible.

**Verdict:** **CONFIRMED / slight upward bias.** 28% defensible as conservative midpoint; ~30% might be better.

**Revision suggestion:** Hold at ~28%, or nudge to **~30%**.

## 17. Lynbrook HS — current ~22%

**Data points:**
- Lynbrook 2022-25 matriculations (4-yr window, ~430/yr × 4 = ~1720 grads):
  - **UC Berkeley 94, UCLA 45, UCSD 35** = 174 T25-UC over 4 yrs = **~43.5/yr / 430 ≈ 10.1% UC-T25**.
  - UCSB 73, UCD 49, UCI 48, UCSC 55, UCR 54 (non-T25 UCs).
- Stanford count not in the published list (likely sub-1/yr based on Bay Area patterns).
- Add non-UC T25 (CMU, USC, Cornell, Michigan, MIT typical for Cupertino-Asian-American profile) → estimate ~3-5pp on top of 10% UC-T25.
- Half of Lynbrook attends UC system; ~25% attends community college (per multiple sources).

**Aggregation:** **~10% UC-T25 + ~3-5% non-UC T25 = ~13-15% total T25.** Current 22% appears **too high by ~7-9pp.**

**Verdict:** **REVISION SUGGESTED** — current 22% looks inflated.

**Revision suggestion:** ~22% → **~14-15%**. (Reconciles directly with FUHSD published 4-year matric data.) Note: this contradicts the Saratoga/Monta Vista comparable estimates which were also UCSD-corrected to ~20%+; the FUHSD per-college table actually argues Lynbrook is **lower** than Monta Vista on UC-T25 per capita (10.1% vs Monta Vista's 20.7% claim — worth re-checking Monta Vista too).

## 18. Monta Vista HS — current ~22%

**Data points:**
- Class of 2024 destinations: 85% UC, 31% CSU (overlap — categorical/multi-app), 9% out-of-state, 5% in-state private, 11% 2-year.
- Current estimate tooltip claims **UCB ~40/yr + UCLA ~25/yr + UCSD ~20/yr ≈ 85 UC-T25 / 410 = 20.7% UC-T25.** If true, this is roughly 2× Lynbrook's per-capita UC-T25 rate (10.1%) — which is suspicious given Lynbrook and Monta Vista have near-identical demographics and academic profile.
- The Monta Vista PDF profile is binary-locked; I couldn't independently re-verify the 20.7% claim.
- More than half attend UC overall (likely 50-55% all UCs).
- Strong CMU, USC, Stanford, Cornell, Northwestern, Michigan presence per anecdotal sources.

**Aggregation:** If Lynbrook's verifiable 4-year FUHSD per-college data shows ~10% UC-T25, Monta Vista's claimed 20.7% UC-T25 is implausibly high relative to the Lynbrook benchmark. Either (a) Monta Vista really is 2× stronger at the UC top end (possible — Monta Vista is slightly larger and more academically intense), or (b) current Monta Vista estimate is over-counting.

**Verdict:** **VERIFY** — high uncertainty. Current 22% is plausible if Monta Vista UCB+UCLA+UCSD is indeed 2× Lynbrook; otherwise likely closer to ~15-17%.

**Revision suggestion:** Hold 22% pending direct read of MVHS24-25SchoolProfileFINAL_2.pdf (manual review needed; auto-fetch returned binary). If MV mirrors Lynbrook's ratio, drop to **~16-17%**.
