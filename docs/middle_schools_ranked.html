Bay Area Middle Schools — Inferred T25 % from HS feeders

Estimated T25 / T10 / T50 / LAC15 / UC matriculation rates for Bay Area middle schools, derived by weighting their primary high-school feeders' T25 estimates by feeder share. K-12 / 6-12 private schools use the school's own HS T25 directly (since the same student body continues). Public middle schools use the weighted-average of their zoned high-school destinations. Confidence is one tier worse than the dominant HS feeder to reflect feeder-weight uncertainty. See the methodology page for the full inference chain and worked examples.

This is a triple-estimation chain — read before drawing conclusions.
  • Selection bias is the dominant signal. Choosing a middle school does not lock in the high-school outcome — public-school families can move zones, magnet placement is competitive, and private-MS families can choose any HS. The MS doesn't cause the T25 outcome; it predicts it only through the families it enrolls.
  • Three layers of estimation stack here. MS feeder weight × HS T25 estimate × matriculation conversion. R-tier rows can be off by ±10pp, and even E-tier rows are noisier than the corresponding HS estimates.
  • Feeder weights drift. District zone boundaries, magnet seat allocations, and choice patterns shift year-over-year. Weights below are the dominant-feeder approximation, not a 5-year average.
  • Catholic / private K-8 mappings are the weakest links — "where our grads went" pages are usually a list, not weighted percentages. Numbers there are R-tier or N-tier.
Confidence: M measured (±3pp; same school's HS data) E estimated (±6pp) R rough (±10pp) N very rough (±15pp)
