Bay Area High Schools — All schools ranked by T25 %

A unified ranking of every Bay Area public, charter, and private high school covered by the two source files, sorted by estimated U.S. News Top-25 National University matriculation rate. T25 % is the average % of a graduating class that matriculates to a T25 school over the most recent 3–5 classes. The companion columns T10 % (HYPSM-tier ultra-elites), T50 % (broader prestige bucket), LAC15 % (top liberal arts colleges), and UC % (all 9 UC campuses combined, capped at 80%) round out the picture — each can be sorted independently by clicking its column header. See methodology for definitions and the per-school-type ratio table. GS is the GreatSchools 1–10 score; Niche is the niche.com overall letter grade.

Read before drawing conclusions.
  • Selection bias dominates. Schools at the top admit students who were already on a strong trajectory. The numbers reflect who they enroll as much as what they teach.
  • R/N rows can be off by ±10 percentage points. Use rough-confidence rankings as a starting point for due diligence, not a final answer.
  • Catholic schools are systematically underweighted on T25 — graduates often prefer Notre Dame, Santa Clara, USF, USD (only Notre Dame is in the T25), so absolute T25 share looks low even at strong, large Catholic schools.
  • GS/Niche are external proxies, not measures of T25 outcome. A high GS or A+ Niche does not imply a high T25 % (e.g., schools with strong UC pipelines but few private-T25 placements often score very highly on both).
Confidence: M measured (±2pp) E estimated (±5pp) R rough (±10pp) N no usable data
