Audit date: 2026-04-26
Auditor: graduate-audit agent (ranks 21-30 slice)
Time-box: 60 min hard
Method: search online graduate profiles (school alumni news, school newspapers, Niche, Reddit, local press, school matriculation pages, Class-of-XX articles, LinkedIn snippets) and collect named graduate-to-college matches. Coverage column legend:
- DP = number of independent data points (named grad-to-college, per-college matric counts, sourced acceptance counts, etc.) collected

### 29. Quarry Lane School (Dublin) — current ~16% / VERDICT: CONFIRM with caveat
- **Class of 2024 official acceptance data (school site):**
  - 78 grads, 689 acceptances from 141 universities
  - T25 acceptances per US News ranking:
    - Stanford 2, Yale 1, Penn 2, Duke 1, JHU 1, Cornell 3, UCB 15, UCLA 7, Michigan 4, Georgetown 1, UNC 2, CMU 6, Emory 1, UVA 1, WashU 1, UCSD 13 → **subtotal ≈ 61 T25 acceptances**
  - Total "Top 50 acceptances" = 256 (per school) — confirms strong T50 tail
- 5.45% of Class of 2024 → HYPSM (per Quarry Lane stated metric)
- 22.73% → Top 25 (acceptance, not matric)
- 11.82% → Top 50 (this is matric-style, school's own framing)

**Conversion check.** 61 T25 acceptances (unique-student count probably lower since strong students cross-admit) → ~40-50 unique-student T25 admits. × 90% STEM-affluent-secular yield (per Sec 8 methodology) → ~36-45 matric / 78 grads = **~46-58% T25 matric**. **This contradicts the school's own stated 22.73% T25** (which is closer to an acceptance-rate metric).

**Caveat.** Quarry Lane's metrics appear to mix acceptance-of-class vs matric-of-class definitions; the 22.73% figure is plausibly acceptance share (% of class admitted to at least one T25), not matric. If true, T25 matric is closer to 11-16% (the school's own T50 figure of 11.82% is a floor on matric). HTML estimate of 16% sits inside this band.

**Verdict.** CONFIRM at ~16%; recommend keeping confidence at R given school's metric ambiguity.
